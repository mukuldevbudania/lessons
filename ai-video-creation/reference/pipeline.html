The AI Brand-Ad Pipeline

The whole workflow on one page. Open-source, DGX-Spark-local, commercial-safe.

Nine stages. The golden rule running through all of them: resolve consistency on cheap still images first; only animate frames you've already locked. Video generation is the slowest, least controllable step — never discover a problem there.

1 Brief→ 2 Script→ 3 Character / Product Sheet→ 4 Shot list→ 5 Keyframe stills→ 6 Image→Video→ 7 Upscale / interpolate→ 8 Edit + sound (Resolve)→ 9 Grade + deliver

Stage by stage

#StageToolOutput artifactWhy it exists
1BriefClaude + you1-pager: brand, audience, one message, tone, CTA, aspect ratio, durationEverything downstream is judged against this. No brief = pretty footage that sells nothing.
2Script / conceptClaudeVoiceover or beat sheet + ad structure (hook→problem→product→payoff)Structure is what makes 20s feel like an ad, not a montage.
3Character / product sheetQwen-Image / FLUX schnell / SDXL + ComfyUITurnaround grid (front/¾/side/back), expressions, consistent lighting; a character LoRA if the hero recursThe single biggest lever on consistency. This is your "casting."
4Shot listClaude + youTable: shot #, size (WS/MS/CU), camera move, duration, description, the still-image promptConverts the script into a checklist of images to generate. Removes improvisation.
5Keyframe stillsImage model + ControlNet/IPAdapter + character LoRAOne (or 2) locked still per shot — the exact composition, matching the sheetCheap, fast, fully controllable. You iterate here, not in video.
6Image → VideoWan 2.2 / HunyuanVideo 1.5 / LTX-Video in ComfyUI3–10s clip per keyframe, with a motion promptAnimates the locked frame. On Spark: batch these overnight.
7Upscale / interpolateComfyUI (upscalers, RIFE/FILM frame interp)1080p, smooth 24–30fps clipsGeneration is often sub-1080p / low-fps to save time; fix in post-gen.
8Edit + soundDaVinci Resolve (free)Timeline: cuts, pacing to the beat sheet, VO, music, SFXThe edit is half the "cinematic" feeling. Pacing sells energy.
9Grade + deliverResolve (Color + Deliver pages)Consistent color across clips (they won't match raw), export per platformA unifying grade hides that shots came from different generations.
Licensing tripwire. A brand ad is commercial. FLUX.1 [dev] is non-commercial — do not ship it. Commercial-safe image: Qwen-Image, FLUX.1 [schnell], SDXL. Commercial-safe video: Wan 2.2, HunyuanVideo 1.5, LTX-Video (all Apache-2.0). Re-check the weight's license card before every delivery. source
DGX Spark reality. 128 GB unified memory means the big models fit; ~273 GB/s bandwidth means they run slowly. Design the workflow for batch: lock stills interactively by day, queue video renders overnight. Don't expect real-time scrubbing. source

The three pillars of consistency

"Cinematic" and "on-brand" both reduce to consistency. Three mechanisms, used together:

MechanismHolds what constantWhere
Character LoRA (fine-tune)A specific face/body/product across any promptStage 3→5
IPAdapter / reference imageIdentity & style, no training neededStage 5
ControlNet (pose/depth/edge)Composition & camera, shot to shotStage 5
Unifying color gradePalette & mood across mismatched clipsStage 9
