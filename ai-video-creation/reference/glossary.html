Glossary

Shared nomenclature. Every lesson uses these terms exactly as defined here.

Models & runners

TermMeaning
ComfyUINode-graph app that runs open image/video models. The orchestration hub of the whole pipeline.
Wan 2.2Alibaba open video model (Apache-2.0). MoE architecture, T2V + I2V, up to ~10s. The default cinematic workhorse.
HunyuanVideo 1.5Tencent open video model (Apache-2.0). 8.3B rebuild runs on consumer VRAM. Strong motion.
LTX-VideoLightricks open video model (Apache-2.0). Fastest of the three; lower ceiling. Good for drafts.
Qwen-ImageAlibaba open image model, 20B, Apache-2.0. Commercial-safe; excellent text-in-image. A top brand-ad pick.
FLUX.1 [schnell]Fast FLUX variant, Apache-2.0 → commercial-OK. (Contrast with [dev].)
FLUX.1 [dev]Highest-quality FLUX, but non-commercial license. ❌ Not for brand work.
SDXLStable Diffusion XL. Deepest LoRA/ControlNet ecosystem. Commercial-OK under Stability Community License (with conditions).

Consistency & control

TermMeaning
LoRALow-Rank Adaptation — a small fine-tune that teaches a base model one specific character, product, or style. Your "casting" of a recurring hero.
ControlNetConditions a generation on structure — a pose skeleton, depth map, or edge map — so composition/camera is controlled, not random.
IPAdapter"Image Prompt" adapter — feeds a reference image for identity/style transfer without training a LoRA. Fast consistency.
Character sheet / turnaroundA reference grid of one character from multiple angles + expressions, under consistent lighting. The source of truth for identity.
KeyframeA locked still for a specific shot — the exact frame you'll animate. Composition is resolved here, cheaply.
SeedThe random number that determines a generation. Fix it to reproduce / vary deliberately.

Film craft (the non-AI half)

TermMeaning
Shot sizesWS wide, MS medium, CU close-up, ECU extreme close-up. Vary them for rhythm.
Beat sheetThe ad's structure as timed beats: hook → problem → product → payoff → CTA.
180° ruleKeep the camera on one side of the action line so screen direction stays consistent between cuts.
Color gradeDeliberate, consistent color/mood applied in post. The thing that makes mismatched AI clips feel like one film.
I2V / T2VImage-to-Video / Text-to-Video. This pipeline is I2V-first (lock the frame, then move it).
