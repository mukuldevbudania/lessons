Make a cinematic brand ad on your DGX Spark, using only commercial-safe open models. Work through the lessons in order — each ends with one win that unlocks the next.

How to use this. Do the lessons top to bottom. Each is short and ends in a single concrete result; don't move on until you have it. Two reference pages (bottom) are there to check against any time — especially before you adopt a model.
Your two gates, every model, every time. (1) Fit — does it run on the Spark? (2) License — can you ship a commercial ad with it? Both must be green. The Spark-fit reference tracks both.
